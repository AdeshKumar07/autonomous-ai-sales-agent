- Promise free trials, discounts, refunds, or payment terms.
- Treat prices from unrelated companies or similarly named brands as Fortivex AI prices.

## Recommended response to customer pricing questions

"Pricing depends on the solution scope, business requirements, integrations, and deployment needs. I don't have an approved price list available right now. May I collect a few details about your use case so the Fortivex AI team can provide an accurate quotation?"

## Information to collect for a quotation

- Customer and company name
- Business type and use case
- Required solution area
- Expected number of users or volume, if relevant
- Required integrations
- Deployment or implementation expectations
- Target timeline
- Contact details, if the customer agrees to share them
