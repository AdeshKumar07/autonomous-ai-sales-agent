# Products & Capabilities (DEMO DATA)

> **DEMO DATA:** All product names, specifications, and metrics below are sample demo data, not official FORTIVEX AI PRIVATE LIMITED product specifications.

## 1. Fortivex Voice Qualifier
- **Purpose:** Real-time AI voice agent for inbound website and phone-style lead qualification.
- **Languages:** English (`en-IN`) and Hindi (`hi-IN`).
- **Key Features:** Live speech-to-text, natural text-to-speech, automated BANT-style discovery (Budget, Authority/Company, Need, Timeline), and automatic HOT/WARM/COLD lead scoring.

## 2. Fortivex Knowledge Assistant (RAG)
- **Purpose:** Answers customer product, pricing, and policy questions grounded in uploaded company documentation.
- **Capabilities:** Semantic search over Markdown/text knowledge bases with source attribution to prevent hallucinated claims.

## 3. Fortivex CRM Sync
- **Purpose:** Stores structured lead profiles and multi-turn conversation history in PostgreSQL for sales team review and follow-up.
